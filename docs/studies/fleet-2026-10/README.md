# Fleet on Head Gasket Bench — October 2026

## Two 18/18 repairs with Fleet 1+4.

The only two full repairs (18/18) in round 2 came from Fleet with one lead and four workers.

**Mean points out of 18: Fleet 1+4, 12.75; Fleet 1+2×2, 11.5; Codex native subagents, 7.5; single agent, 5.75.**

Sixteen runs, four per setup, on one deterministic V1 task in Head Gasket Bench 0.1.3, with enforced lead-only finish. All runs requested gpt-6-luna at max reasoning effort through Codex CLI 0.162.1; requested model labels do not independently attest served weights.

The benchmark environment and independent scorer agreed on all 16 round-2 scores. **Four repetitions of one task cannot rank the arms.**

Team size, delegation instructions, context packaging and tool surfaces differ together, so these configurations do not isolate the effect of transport.

Round 1 had no full repairs in eight runs; FB2 scored 9/18 in the environment and 8/18 with the independent scorer because of a CP07 disagreement. The rounds use different finish protocols and remain separate exploratory cohorts.

A full repair here means all twelve benchmark checkpoints passed; it does not establish physical repair competence.
