The public pins differ from the sealed pins only because of the substitutions described in `../RECORDS.md`, section 5 (two internal shared-folder spellings of benchmark policy documents replaced by neutral labels; the digests that cited a printed service manual and two private citation records withheld; the reviewed reference script re-pinned to the corrected corpus digest; three derived digests recomputed over the published inputs). The five files are byte-identical to `../../env/configs/` and are regenerated byte for byte by `python3 -m headgasket prepare` there. The byte-identical V1 configuration was used by both runtimes (the hardened epoch kept the accepted amended configurations unchanged), so one public V1 file serves both.
